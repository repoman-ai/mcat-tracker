## 2. Information density — what to cut

The hierarchy is right: Today answers "what now", everything else is one tap away. The problem is
that each screen then repeats itself, so the answer arrives three or four times and the one
actionable copy competes with the others.

### D-01 · HIGH · The assignment heading is clipped and the hidden half is unrecoverable

**Where:** `css/styles.css:172`

```css
.today-action h2 { … -webkit-line-clamp: 3; overflow: hidden; … }
```

**Evidence.** Measured at 390 px on today's real row: `clientHeight` **72 px**, `scrollHeight`
**144 px** — exactly half the text is clipped. Today's assignment is:

> General Chemistry Ch. 4 - Compounds and Stoichiometry; Critical Analysis and Reasoning Skills
> Ch. 1 - About CARS; Critical Analysis and Reasoning Skills Ch. 2 - Analyzing Rhetoric

The reader sees up to `…Critical Analys…`. There is no `title` attribute on the `h2`, so hovering
reveals nothing, and the only route to the full string is opening **Review assignment**.

**Fix.** Do not simply unclamp — a six-line heading at `clamp(1.4rem, 5vw, 2.6rem)` is worse. Shorten
what the heading *carries*. Build a short day label from the row and let the checklist below carry
the chapter names, which it already does in full:

- `"3 chapters · Rapid review"`, or
- the week focus already in the data (`"Fast math + Gen Chem foundations"`), or
- the first chapter plus a count (`"General Chemistry Ch. 4 + 2 more"`)

Keep the full assignment string as the `title`/`aria-label` and in the dialog.

### D-02 · HIGH · Today states each fact three or four times

**Where:** `js/views/today.js:142` (`.chapter-line`), `:143` (`.today-facts`), `taskChecklist` in
`js/views/shared.js:105`, plus `row.sourceNotes` rendered at `js/views/today.js:141`.

**Evidence — chapter names, three times on one screen:**

1. the `h2` (full assignment string)
2. five checklist steps — `Review GC04 · Compounds and Stoichiometry`, `Review CARS01 · About CARS`, …
3. `.chapter-line` — `GC04 Compounds and Stoichiometry · CARS01 About CARS · CARS02 Analyzing Rhetoric`

**Evidence — practice targets, three times:**

1. two checklist steps — `Complete 8 UWorld topic questions`, `Complete 1 CARS passage`
2. the `Practice` and `CARS` cells of `.today-facts`
3. inside the guardrails paragraph — *"…+ 8 UWorld with review + 1 CARS passage + 15-30 min maintenance…"*

The card measures **1,242 px** tall at 390 px wide — roughly 1.5 phone screens for a single day.
Full page height on Today at 390 px is **2,901 px**.

**Fix.** The checklist is the only copy you can act on. Keep it. Then:

- delete `.chapter-line` (`today.js:142`) — every chapter ID and title is already in the checklist
- move `.today-facts` (`today.js:143`) behind **Review assignment**, where the identical
  `detail-list--grid` table already exists in `assignmentDetailHTML`
- keep the guardrails aside, but consider trimming the duplicated quota clause from `sourceNotes`
  at generation time in `scripts/build_mcat_tracker.mjs` rather than in the view

Net effect: ~300 px shorter, no fact lost.

**Where:** `js/views/today.js:28–39` (`countdown`)

**Evidence.** `141 days` renders in the display serif as the most prominent figure on the screen,

**Fix.** While `registeredExamDate` is empty, render one quiet line —

### D-05 · MEDIUM · An empty Log screen stacks ten separate "nothing here" messages

**Where:** `js/views/log.js:242` (`.log-overview`), plus the repair and pattern panels

**Evidence.** On `#log/repair` with no entries, in order down the page:

2–5. four zero tiles — `Entries 0`, `Retests overdue 0`, `Repeated topics 0`, `Mastery rated 0/40`
6. `No retests due today`
7–10. four panels each reading `No entries yet.` (Error types, Topics, Sections, Sources)
11. `No repeated topic yet this week`

The first real screen a new user meets is ten statements of absence. The tiles also render on

### D-06 · LOW · Plan opens with four competing ways to navigate, before any schedule

**Where:** `js/views/plan.js:141–142` (header buttons), `:104` (`pastDueSection` jump), `:23` (phase map link)

**Evidence.** Above the first week card: `Jump to week 1`, `Plan guidance`, `Jump to current week`,

**Fix.** One jump control (the past-due card's is better placed and better labelled), one guidance

### D-07 · LOW · Guide gives ten sections three parallel navigations

**Where:** `js/views/guide.js:62`

**Evidence.** A search field, a horizontally scrolling `.guide-section-nav` chip row naming all ten
sections, four "quick jump" cards pointing into four of them, then the ten-section accordion. The
chips and the cards are both indexes of the list immediately below them.

**Fix.** Keep search and the accordion. The quick-jump cards earn their place only because they

### D-08 · LOW · Mastery repeats a null status 40 times

**Where:** `js/views/log.js:212`, legend at `:205`

**Evidence.** Every topic row carries `No exact related mistakes · never reviewed`. With nothing
logged that is 40 identical lines of nothing. The `0 unfamiliar · 1 recognize · 2 explain · 3 apply`

**Fix.** Render the meta `<p>` only when there is something to report (`relatedMistakes.length ||
user.lastReviewed`). Move the legend directly above the first row, or label the buttons at
`min-width: 700px` where `.mastery-row` already switches to a three-column grid
(`css/styles.css:~671`).

## 3. Performance

### P-01 · HIGH · Plan builds the whole book to show one page

**Where:** `js/views/plan.js:54` — `dayCard()` calls `assignmentDetailHTML(row, …)`
unconditionally; `weekCard()` (`:60`) runs it for all twenty weeks.

Every day's chapter subsections, study-mode copy, status `<select>`, two number inputs and notes
`<textarea>` exist in the DOM whether or not that `<details>` is open.

**Measured on `#plan`, no filters:**

| Metric | Value | Consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| DOM nodes | 16,697 | Memory and style recalc on every interaction |
| `#view-root` innerHTML | 940 KB | Re-parsed in full on each render |
| `<textarea>` elements | 145 | One per day, all hidden |
| `<select>` elements | 149 | Same |
| Synchronous re-render | 194 ms | Blocking, on every filter change **and every checkbox tap** |

`bindCompletionButtons` → `context.updateState` → `renderCurrent()` → full `renderPlan`. 194 ms on
this desktop is comfortably 500 ms–1.5 s on a mid-range phone: a visible stall between tapping a
checkbox and seeing it tick. The `captureViewState` machinery in `js/view-state.js` exists precisely
because the rebuild is total.

**Fix — pick one:**

**(a) Lazy detail — bigger win.** Render only the `<summary>` in `dayCard`. On the `toggle` event,
inject `assignmentDetailHTML(row, context.data, context.state)` into that one `<details>` and call
`bindAssignmentDetail` / `bindTaskChecklist` / `bindCompletionButtons` scoped to it. Drops the idle
view to roughly a tenth of its nodes and shrinks what `captureViewState` has to restore.

**(b) Surgical update — smaller change.** Keep the full render, but on a completion toggle patch
only that day's `<summary>` (checkbox state, `n/m steps`) and its week's `n/7` score, instead of
calling `context.rerender`.

## 4. Defects and dead ends

### B-01 · HIGH · Checking "Complete 8 UWorld topic questions" credits zero questions

**Where:** `js/views/today.js:101` reads `actualQuestions`; `js/daily.js:83` (`withDailyTask`) and
`:105` (`withDailyCompletion`) never write it. Only `bindAssignmentDetail`'s **Save day** handler
(`js/views/shared.js:~236`) sets it.

**Evidence.** I completed all five checklist steps for 3 Sep. The sidebar then read:

```
Study days complete          1/7
Practice questions complete  0/24
CARS passages complete       0/13
```

Two of the three weekly meters are permanently zero for anyone who uses the checklist as designed.

**Fix.** When a `practice:` task is toggled in `withDailyTask`, default the day's `actualQuestions` /
`actualCars` from `countPracticeQuestions(row.practiceTarget)` and `row.carsPassages`; clear them
when unchecked. Same in `withDailyCompletion`. Leave the detail form authoritative so a short day can
still be corrected downward.

**Do this before the celebration work** — the Tier 2 panel quotes these numbers, and right now it
would announce `0 of 24`.

---

### B-02 · MEDIUM · The focus timer drifts, and stops outright when the phone locks

**Where:** `js/views/today.js:222–226`, saved duration at `:231`

```js
focus.timer = setInterval(() => {
  focus.remaining = Math.max(0, focus.remaining - 1);
  …
}, 1000);
```

**Evidence.** `remaining` is decremented once per tick. Backgrounded tabs throttle `setInterval` to a
fraction of a hertz; a locked iPhone stops it. The saved session inherits the error:
`minutes = Math.round((25 * 60 - focus.remaining) / 60)`. A real 25-minute block taken with the
screen off records as a few minutes.

**Fix.** Keep `startedAt` plus accumulated paused milliseconds and derive `remaining` from
`Date.now()`; the interval becomes a repaint only. The same change fixes the recorded duration.

---

### B-03 · MEDIUM · Log tabs scroll off-screen on a phone with the scrollbar hidden

**Where:** `css/styles.css:414` — `overflow-x: auto; scrollbar-width: none;` plus
`.log-tabs::-webkit-scrollbar { display: none; }` at `:415`. No `scrollIntoView` after render in
`js/views/log.js`.

**Evidence.** At 390 px only **Capture**, **Repair** and **Entries** fit. Navigating to
`#log/mastery` leaves the active tab off-screen: no highlighted tab visible, and no hint that
**Mastery** and **Export** exist. Same pattern in `.phase-track` (`styles.css:319`) for later weeks
on Plan, and in `.guide-section-nav`.

**Fix.** After render, `container.querySelector('.log-tabs [aria-current="page"]')
?.scrollIntoView({ inline: "center", block: "nearest" })`. Add a right-edge mask so the overflow is
visible: `mask-image: linear-gradient(90deg, #000 85%, transparent)`, removed at scroll end. Apply
the same treatment to `.phase-track` and `.guide-section-nav`.

---

### B-04 · LOW · Focus sessions are write-only

**Where:** written at `js/views/today.js:231–235`; read nowhere.

**Evidence.** `state.focusSessions` is saved, merged across devices, counted in the import summary
(`js/views/log.js:292`) and written to JSON backups — and nothing in the interface ever reads it
back. Finish a 25-minute block and the record disappears from view permanently.

**Fix.** Surface it or stop storing it. The **Completed** tab is the natural home: total focused time
and this week's. See §6 — the celebration also wants somewhere to put finished work.

---

### B-05 · LOW · `settings.reducedMotionOverride` is stored, synced, exported — and never read

**Where:** `js/storage.js:21`. `grep -rn reducedMotionOverride js/ css/` returns exactly that one
line plus four backup fixtures under `tests/output/`.

**Fix.** Wire it as the celebration control (§6). The field, sync path, merge rules and backup format
already exist; only a control and a read are missing.

---

### B-06 · LOW · The unscored diagnostic asks for scaled scores

**Where:** `js/views/exams.js:56` already knows (`exam.diagnostic ? "Unscored" : …`), but the form at
`:59–67` renders C/P, CARS, B/B, P/S and `Total score` with `min="472" max="528"` regardless.

**Evidence.** The AAMC Unscored Sample is listed as **Unscored**, then offers five scaled-score
fields. There is no scaled score to enter, so the field either stays empty — leaving the trend chart
flat through the plan's first evidence point — or gets filled with something incomparable.

**Fix.** Branch on `exam.diagnostic`: relabel the four section inputs as percent correct (or raw
correct/total) and hide `Total`. Exclude diagnostics from the scaled-score trend line while still
counting them toward `0/8 exams complete`.

---

### B-07 · LOW · Quick capture is desktop-only on a phone-first app

**Where:** `css/styles.css:85` — `.topbar-log { display: none; }`, restored only at `:657` inside the
`min-width: 960px` block.

**Evidence.** On a phone the routes to capture are the Log tab, or the `Log a question` button at
`js/views/today.js:150` — roughly 1,000 px down the Today card. The capture form itself declares nine
required fields (`date, result, source, section, topic, error type, cause, reasoning, and fix`),
which is the right discipline for review and the wrong friction for the moment mid-block when you
actually miss a question.

**Fix.** Move `Log a question` up beside the completion buttons on the Today card. Consider a
two-stage capture: date/source/section/topic saves immediately as *needs repair*; cause, reasoning
and fix become required to mark it *repaired*. Keeps the standard without losing entries to friction.

## 5. Shape of the day

### S-01 · MEDIUM · Nothing changes when the day is done

**Where:** `js/views/today.js:124–152`

**Evidence.** After the fifth step the eyebrow becomes `Today is complete`, the status badge reads
`Complete`, and the button becomes `Completed`. Everything else is unchanged: the full checklist, the
guardrails paragraph telling you how long to spend, the resource/mode/practice table, the week
milestone, the focus timer still offering to start a block. The two past-due days remain below,
unpromoted. The card is still 1,242 px.

**Fix.** On completion, collapse the card to a summary strip — assignment, `5/5 steps`, workload
estimate, `Review what you logged` — and promote what is actually next: the past-due card if rows
remain, otherwise a quiet preview of tomorrow's block. The screen should answer "what now" again,
which is the app's stated thesis.

This is the finding the celebration request is really about, and it is worth doing whether or not
any animation ships.

## 6. Completion celebration — design

### The problem, precisely

Ticking the fifth of five steps produces *exactly* the same feedback as ticking the first: a small
dark toast, bottom-right, reading `Completed: Complete 1 CARS passage`, with an Undo button. Both
paths run through `context.showToast(...)` in `js/views/shared.js:138`. The app's largest moment is
indistinguishable from its smallest.

The fix is not "add confetti" — it is to make the reward proportional, in three tiers.

---

### Tier 1 — a step is checked (~5× per day)

Stays quiet. Everything that already happens (strikethrough, green box, toast with Undo, `n/5 done`)
plus just enough physicality that the tap feels registered on a phone.

- **Box stamp** — 120 ms scale `1 → 1.18 → 1` on `.task-check__box` only,
  `cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .3, 1.2)`. Nothing else moves.
- **Counter tick** — the `0/5 done` figure translates up 3 px and flashes to the accent over 200 ms.
- **Haptic** — `navigator.vibrate(10)`. Real on Android, a silent no-op on iOS.
- **No particles, no sound.** Five bursts a day is five interruptions a day.

---

### Tier 2 — the day completes (1× per day) — *the requested moment*

Fires only on the `not-complete → complete` edge, reached either by the last checklist item or by the
**Mark complete** button. Replaces the toast with a panel rendered in place of the action card's top
section, so the celebration and the answer to "what now" are the same object.

- **Check draws in** — an SVG tick animated with `stroke-dasharray` over 400 ms; the card border and
  ground transition to the completion tone underneath it.
- **One burst, then gone** — ~80 particles on a single `<canvas>`, 1.0–1.4 s, anchored to the tick,
  element removed on the last frame.
- **Undo moves into the panel** so the existing 10-second undo window survives the toast being
  replaced. `js/toast.js` binds `Ctrl/⌘Z` to `undoButton.click()` — keep that reference pointing at
  the panel's button.
- **Haptic** — `navigator.vibrate([0, 25, 50, 25])`.
- **Announced once** through the existing `[data-toast]` `role="status"` region:
  `"Today complete. Week 1, 2 of 7 days."` The canvas is `aria-hidden="true"` and
  `pointer-events: none`.

**Panel copy — real values from today's state:**

```
✓ Day complete
Week 1 · 2 of 7 study days · 2-day streak
8 of 24 practice questions this week · 1 of 13 CARS passages
Tomorrow — Light formula/unit recall + diagnostic logistics

[ 2 past-due days → ]  [ Log a question ]  [ Undo ]
```

The primary action is conditional: past-due rows if any exist, otherwise `Log a question`, and on a
clean board simply `See tomorrow`. Praise on its own goes stale within a week; a number that moved
does not.

---

### Tier 3 — a week or milestone closes (~weekly)

All study days in a week complete, or a full-length review day finishes. Same machinery, longer
burst, and the panel carries what the plan already knows: `row.weeklyMilestone`
(*"AAMC Unscored Sample Sat Sep 5; review Sun-Mon; no catch-up debt"*) and the next entry from
`data.phaseMap`. Having a real Tier 3 is what lets Tier 2 stay modest.

---

### Where it fires

| Surface | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Full Tier 2 panel, in place of the action card's top. |
| **Plan** | Box stamp plus a richer toast only — `Sep 1 complete · 1 past-due day left`. A full-screen burst mid-scroll of a 145-row list is the wrong register, and see P-01 before adding anything to that render path. |
| **Past-due rows on Today** | Same as Plan. Clearing debt deserves the toast, not the fanfare. |

### Where it must **not** fire

- Undo, and re-completion immediately after undo
- `applySyncedState` (`js/app.js:~57`) — a completion arriving from your other phone
- JSON import, which can mark dozens of days complete at once
- The day-detail **Status** dropdown set to `Complete` — deliberate bookkeeping, often for an old
  day, not a finish

---

### Streak — derived, not stored

No schema change, no migration, no sync conflict. It falls out of daily records that already sync.
Rest days pass through rather than breaking the run.

```js
// js/data.js
export function completionStreak(data, state, today = todayISO()) {
  let streak = 0;
  for (let i = data.schedule.length - 1; i >= 0; i -= 1) {
    const row = data.schedule[i];
    if (row.date > today) continue;
    if (!isStudyRow(row)) continue;                        // rest days pass through
    if (state.daily[row.id]?.status !== "complete") break; // first gap ends it
    streak += 1;
  }
  return streak;
}
```

---

### Where the code goes

Both sites that can complete a day already compute `previous` / `wasComplete`, `previousRecord` and
`next`, so edge detection is one line each.

```js
// js/views/shared.js — bindCompletionButtons (~line 28) and bindTaskChecklist (~line 133)
const becameComplete = previous !== "complete"
  && next.daily[id].status === "complete";
if (context.updateState(next) === false) return;

if (becameComplete) context.celebrate("day", { row, undo: undoAction });
else context.showToast(message, "success", undoAction);
```

- **New module `js/celebrate.js`** exporting `celebrate(level, payload)` — roughly 80 lines, no
  dependency, consistent with the project's no-build-step constraint.
- **One `<canvas>`**, `position: fixed; inset: 0; pointer-events: none; z-index: 90`, appended to
  `<body>` and removed when the animation ends. Prefer canvas over 80 absolutely-positioned spans: a
  phone in the middle of the Plan view is exactly where DOM particles would stutter.
- **`context` gains `celebrate`** next to `showToast` in `js/app.js`, so views stay declarative and
  the module stays unit-testable.
- **Tests.** `tests/checklist.test.mjs` already covers `withDailyTask`; add three cases — the edge
  fires once, `complete → complete` does not fire, undo does not fire.

---

### Motion budget and the opt-out

- **Honour `prefers-reduced-motion` properly.** The block at `css/styles.css:675` collapses all
  durations to `0.01ms`, which would run the burst invisibly at full cost. Check
  `matchMedia("(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)")` inside `celebrate()` and skip the canvas
  entirely; the panel, the copy and the screen-reader announcement still happen.
- **Give `settings.reducedMotionOverride` a control at last** (B-05), in the account dialog:
  *System* / *Always celebrate* / *Never*. `never` reduces Tier 2 to a plain success toast; `always`
  runs the animation even when the OS asks for reduced motion.
- **Never block the tap.** Run the burst *after* the state write, in the same frame the panel
  renders. Total budget under 1.4 s.
- **Sound off by default.** This app gets used in libraries. If wanted, a single short WebAudio tone
  behind a fourth setting value — never an audio file.

---

### What not to build

- **No full-screen takeover requiring dismissal.** It interrupts the very next action, which on
  today's board is "start one of the two past-due days".
- **No badges or trophy state.** Another synced schema for something the schedule already implies,
  and it competes with the 0–3 mastery scale that is already the app's measure of progress.
- **No streak-loss warnings, no guilt copy.** The app handles unfinished work as *past due, oldest
  first, defer if needed* without shaming, and today's own guardrail text reads *"never rush to tick
  boxes."* A celebration that punishes a missed day contradicts the plan it is celebrating.

---

## 7. Suggested order of work

1. **B-01 — practice counts.** Half the weekly meters read zero forever, and the Tier 2 panel quotes
   them. Do this first or the celebration announces `0 of 24`.
2. **D-01 + D-02 — the Today card.** Unclip the heading by shortening it; drop `.chapter-line`; move
   `.today-facts` into the dialog. ~300 px off the card, no information lost.
3. **Celebration Tiers 1 and 2.** The card is now short enough that the completion panel lands
   somewhere visible.
4. **S-01 — the completed-day layout.** Follows naturally; the panel and the collapsed summary are
   the same redesign.
5. **P-01 — lazy Plan details.** Independent of the above, and the largest single quality gain on a
   phone.
6. **D-03 → D-08 — density trims.** Small, isolated, each safe on its own.
7. **B-02 → B-07 — remaining defects**, plus Tier 3 once Tier 2 has settled.
