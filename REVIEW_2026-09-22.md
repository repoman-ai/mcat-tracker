# Website and schedule review September 22 2026

## Changes applied

- Six exam weeks had no rest day despite the guide promising weekly rest. All seven full-length weeks now protect Friday, Saturday's exam, and Sunday–Monday review. New Year's Day was already protected. The generator now rejects any week without rest.
- The diagnostic week's science questions are redistributed from 3/3/2/2 across Tuesday–Friday to 4/3/3 across Tuesday–Thursday. The other affected Fridays carried no question quota. The plan still contains 222 UWorld science questions, 360 Section Bank questions, all 83 chapters accounted for, 182 continuous dates, and seven full-lengths with two review days each. Chapter and exam dates did not move.
- Weekly momentum now includes partial checklist progress, a seven-day schedule navigator, and encouragement based on saved progress. Rest days display recovery guidance instead of a prompt to start studying. Actual question counts remain independent of completion.
- Today exposes the weekly time ceiling and estimated workload risk. The new rest days reduce six exam weeks' workload, but the seven exam-week midpoints remain approximately 20.1–20.9 hours against 20-hour ceilings. Protect review and rest; reduce new work if actual time runs long. Estimates remain advisory and are not artificially capped.
- Invalid dates such as February 30 no longer roll into the next month. Malformed percent-encoded deep links no longer crash routing or Plan navigation.
- Saving a registered date still changes only the countdown. When it differs from the plan, Today and Exams explicitly flag the need to realign exams, review days, and the taper.
- Refreshed entry-point and view cache versions for the release.
