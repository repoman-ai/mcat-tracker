## Evidence and application

Kaplan rotates subjects, integrates topic-matched questions early and targets initial review completion in month three. Its suggested hours and late weekly exam cadence are higher than this plan permits; we use its sequencing principles without copying that volume. [Kaplan six-month plan](https://www.kaptest.com/study/mcat/how-to-study-for-the-mcat-in-6-months/).

Princeton organizes work into specific subtopics with drills/passages, recommends mixing subjects, reviewing tests, and reassessing on roughly a two-to-three-week exam cycle. Its passage/review time estimates support budgeting both activities. Its public samples are not evidence of a particular proprietary course chapter order. [Princeton planning guidance](https://ws.princetonreview.com/med-school-advice/mcat-study-plan), [realistic schedules and rest](https://www.princetonreview.com/med-school-advice/mcat-study-guide/mcat-study-schedule).

AAMC recommends using practice performance to choose content repairs and reviewing incorrect/flagged answers. The plan retains an early diagnostic and two review days per full length. Review all answers briefly and analyze misses, flags and guesses more deeply. [AAMC study planning](https://students-residents.aamc.org/prepare-mcat-exam/creating-your-mcat-exam-study-plan).

Dunlosky and colleagues rated practice testing and distributed practice highly; interleaving had more qualified support. This supports closed-book retrieval with feedback and revisits. It does not establish that this exact interval series, or merely alternating book names, guarantees a score. Begin with a coherent topic explanation, then compare related problem types. [Research review](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/pspi/1529100612453266/).

The Reddit discussion cautions against saving all recall until reading ends, missing rest days and creating an inflexible six-month calendar. Those are useful experience reports, not controlled evidence. We do not import its large question/card quotas or score claims. [Reddit discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Mcat/comments/1alg31j/6_month_study_plan/).
